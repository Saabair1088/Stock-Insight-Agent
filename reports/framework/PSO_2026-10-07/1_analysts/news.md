### News Analyst - macro and event tape

The FY26 print (25 Sep) was read as 'profit surged 122%' in the headline and '-28%' in the standalone detail - both true, and the market chose to focus on the second. The 20 Oct ex-dividend, the 26 Oct MPC and the Q1 FY27 board meeting are the three scheduled events inside the window.

**The macro tape.**

- **SBP on hold at 11.5%** - No change since the 100bp hike of 27 Apr 2026; the 26 Oct MPC is the next binary and the money market leans hawkish (12M KIBOR 12.68%).
- **Inflation 10.26% y/y in September** - Above the 5-7% target band, which keeps the policy rate high and squeezes fuel demand.
- **Crude has been violent: a Middle-East escalation and the temporary Strait of Hormuz closure in Q3FY26, then a retreat that cost PSO its Q4 inventory losses** - Oil was quoted around $101-103/bbl in early October; for an OMC, flat-to-rising prices are inventory-neutral while sharp falls trigger write-downs.
- **Reserves $21.44bn with 3+ months of import cover** - Import cover removes the 2022-23 style FX-shortage tail risk for a company that imports most of its product.
- **Politics dominates the index** - The government-PTI standoff and the protest marches knocked the KSE-100 12% off its high; energy names are ~25% of index weight.
