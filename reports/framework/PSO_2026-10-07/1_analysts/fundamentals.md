### Fundamentals Analyst - valuation and earnings

**Where it stands.** Pakistan's largest fuel marketer: petrol, diesel, jet fuel, lubricants, LPG and LNG, plus a 63.6% stake in Pakistan Refinery Limited.

**Valuation.** At PKR 355.86 the market capitalisation is ~PKR 167.1bn
(469.5mn shares outstanding).
**6.55x** trailing earnings (5.97x forward) - **0.60x** book value per share of PKR 592.43 - a PKR 10.00 dividend is a **2.81%** yield - consensus target **PKR 697.00** (+96%) from 6 analysts (Strong Buy) - beta 0.46.

**Composite fundamentals score: 5.64/10.** This is a book-value story: 0.58x book against a 6.6x multiple of group earnings, but the earnings are lumpy, tax-heavy and hostage to inventory timing. The balance-sheet claim on the state (Rs 415bn of receivables) is both the reason the multiple is low and the reason it should be.

**What the latest filings say.**
- FY26 standalone profit fell 28% to Rs 15.07bn (EPS 32.10 from 44.54) after a ~Rs 23bn net loss in Q4 - inventory losses as fuel prices fell and only 6 LNG cargoes versus 28 a year earlier. *(BR Research / PSX annual financials, 2026-09-28)*
- Group profit attributable to PSO rose 55% to Rs 25.49bn (EPS 54.29); gross profit rose 3% to Rs 99.9bn and gross profit excluding LNG rose 20.5% to Rs 81.9bn. *(PSO FY26 results / Business Recorder, 2026-09-25)*
- Trade receivables fell to Rs 414.8bn from Rs 437.5bn, with SNGPL alone down Rs 34.3bn; lower discount rates cut finance costs 24%. *(PSO FY26 results release, 2026-09-25)*
- Final dividend of Rs 10/share approved for FY26 (ex-date 20 Oct 2026, paid 19 Nov) - the fourth consecutive year at Rs 10. *(PSO board decision / stockanalysis.com, 2026-09-25)*
